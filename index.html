QuickStall

A point-of-sale and order-management app for shops, stalls and kiosks.

Manage products, stock, bills and order history on your Android device. QuickStall also supports local synchronization between configured Billing and Delivery devices, CSV tools and local backups. Feature availability depends on your plan.

View on Google Play

Your records, on your device

QuickStall stores business records locally. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not operate its own cloud backup server.

Optional Google Drive backups

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Google asks you to select an account and authorize access to QuickStall’s private application-data area. QuickStall uses this access to create, list, download and manage its own backups—not to browse your ordinary Drive files.

Backups may include products, inventory, orders, optional order references and business settings. Password-protected backups are encrypted by QuickStall before upload. Google-account-only backups rely on Google account access and Drive protections; they are not end-to-end encrypted by QuickStall.

Cloud-backup creation requires QuickStall Pro or a qualifying verified lifetime entitlement. Restoring an existing cloud backup remains available without Pro. Availability also depends on Google authorization and account policies.
